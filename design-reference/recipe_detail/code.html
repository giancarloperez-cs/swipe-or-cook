Let's Cook

01

Boil a large pot of salted water. Add the penne and cook according to package instructions until al dente.

02

Reserve a half-cup of pasta water, then drain the rest. Toss the hot pasta with the basil pesto in the same pot.

03

Stir in reserved water slowly until creamy. Top with a mountain of parmesan and serve immediately.
